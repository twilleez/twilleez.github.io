🎯 Elite Progression Rules

Strength: complete all prescribed reps with clean technique and RPE ≤8 → modest load increase next exposure. RPE 9+, missed reps or technique loss → repeat or reduce.

Explosive work: full recovery between high-output reps. End or reduce the block if sprint/jump performance clearly deteriorates from today's best.

Basketball practice and games count as training load. On high basketball-load days, remove overlapping conditioning before adding more work.
